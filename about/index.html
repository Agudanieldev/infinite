About Infinite Shares

Infinite Shares, a global real estate investment and brokerage firm, has joined forces with TradeZanga, a cutting-edge digital trading investment company, to create a powerful, diversified platform at the intersection of real estate and digital finance. This strategic merger brings together over nine years of proven success in real estate with the speed, innovation, and scalability of digital investment technologies.

Since 2014, Infinite Shares has raised over $600 million across 15 funds from more than 4,000 accredited and non-accredited investors. Our real estate portfolio includes 2,718 apartment units across North America, Europe, and Asia—delivering consistent returns and long-term growth. TradeZanga, known for its algorithmic trading models and data-driven strategies, adds a layer of dynamic capital deployment and global financial access to our already robust infrastructure.

As one entity, we provide our clients with access to high-performing real estate assets and agile digital investment opportunities—all underpinned by advanced analytics, transparency, and investor-focused service. Whether you're looking to grow your portfolio, enter global markets, or diversify across asset classes, our combined expertise ensures you have the tools, insights, and network to succeed.

We operate with a unified mission: to deliver real results through innovation, integrity, and strategic collaboration. Together, Infinite Shares and TradeZanga are redefining how real estate and digital investments intersect, creating a future where smart capital meets scalable opportunity.

  • Excellence: We are committed to delivering outstanding results and constantly aim to surpass expectations.
  • Ethics: We operate with unwavering honesty and fairness, maintaining the highest standards in every engagement.
  • Responsiveness: We adapt swiftly and efficiently to our clients' changing goals and market demands.
  • Boldness: We back our clients with confident, well-informed decisions, even when the stakes are high.
  • Dignity and Reliability: We treat everyone with respect and build lasting trust through openness and consistency.
  • Passion: We infuse our work with enthusiasm and positivity, creating meaningful connections and a vibrant culture.
